My Favourite Pokémon Cards

Milcery

A cream Pokémon known for its sweet and gentle nature.

Milcery is known to evolve into Alcremie when it is exposed to certain conditions, such as spinning while holding a Sweet item. It is often found in areas abundant with flowers and is said to have a sweet aroma that attracts other Pokémon.
Mantyke

A friendly Water-type Pokémon that glides gracefully through the ocean.

Mantyke is often seen swimming alongside boats and is known to be very social, frequently forming groups with other Mantyke. It evolves into Mantine when leveled up while knowing the move Water Sport.
Sylveon

A graceful Fairy-type Pokémon known for its ribbon-like feelers.

Sylveon is one of the evolutions of Eevee and is known for its affectionate nature. It uses its ribbon-like feelers to emit a soothing aura that can calm conflicts and bring peace to those around it.
